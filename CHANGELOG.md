# Changelog

## 1.1.0 (2026-11-10)

Interface update.

- Hand guide frame and live tips (move closer, move back, keep your hand in view). Badly placed hands no longer type.
- "Typed" flash, a strip of recently typed signs, and "Also possible" next-best guesses.
- Help screen (H), shown automatically until you've trained your own signs.
- Pause (P), so resting hands don't type.
- Recording screen: REC indicator, sample counter and progress across all signs.

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-10)

First version.

- Real-time ASL fingerspelling (24 letters) plus I love you, Good, Space and Delete.
- MediaPipe hand tracking, normalised landmark features, Random Forest classifier.
- Built-in gestures that work without training.
- Guided sign recording, training report with confusion matrix.
- Live captions, offline text-to-speech, saved transcripts.
